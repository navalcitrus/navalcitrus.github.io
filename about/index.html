I am Naval Citrus; perhaps this system is Naval Citrus.

Welcome to my beacon in the vast sea.

I enjoy finding the underlying structure of things and generalizing from there, ideally through play, the way puzzles and cryptography invite you to. Broadly, I hope to write about computation, communication, puzzles, privacy, games, tea, and mathematics.

I have a lot to learn and quite a distance to grow. I hope to illuminate part of that journey and, by laying out a path, shorten the time others take to travel it. If these topics are of interest to you, you may want to read my blog – although it’s still quite empty.
